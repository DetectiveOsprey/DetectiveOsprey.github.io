tips and substitutions

if you still want to make this... why.
but if you do for some reason:

  • if you don't have any key lime juice, you should use one part lime juice and one part lemon juice.
    this will make it extra sour. why do you enjoy eating this??
  • you might think you have to make the crust yourself. you don't.
    making the crust yourself presents a very serious danger, which is that you will eat all of the crust mix before
    you even bake it because it is just so tasty.
    you have been warned.

ingredients

ingredient amount
egg yolks four
sweetened condensed milk fourteen ounces
key lime juice one half cup
cream of tartar one half teaspoon
graham cracker crust one nine-inch crust
whipped cream as much as you want


instructions

  1. preheat the oven to 325 degrees fahrenheit freedom units.
    (because my humans don't use metric like the smarter humans for some reason)
  2. beat the egg yolks on high speed until thick and light in color.
    (how long is that for?? i have no idea)
  3. add the condensed milk and mix on low speed until combined.
  4. while mixing, add half of the gross sour juice, and then the cream of tartar, and
    then half the lime juice. mix in between all of these additions.
    (wow, this sounds hard. makes me wish i had the thumbs for this.)
  5. mix well until completely combined.
  6. pour into pie crust.
    (if you spill it, i won't lick it off the floor for you. you're on your own.)
  7. bake for 10 - 15 minutes.
  8. freeze for at least 3 hours.
  9. put whipped cream on it.
  10. i guess you eat it after that, i'm not sure why you would though.
    have i already said this sounds gross?

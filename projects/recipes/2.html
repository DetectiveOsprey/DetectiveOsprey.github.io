tips and substitutions

  • make sure to spread butter thinly so that it will toast well.
  • check the bread frequently because it will toast much quicker then you think it will.
    remember, if you're a human making this for your dog, they don't want to eat bread if it's burned.
    (well. i might, but they probably won't.)

ingredients

ingredient amount
butter five tablespoons
whole wheat bread eight slices
american cheese eight slices


servings: 4 sandwiches prep time: 20 - 30 min


instructions

  1. soften the butter in the microwave. (do not melt)
  2. place bread on a plate or cutting board.
  3. spread butter on one side of each slice of bread.
  4. place griddle on medium heat on the stove.
  5. when the griddle is warm, pour the extra butter onto it.
  6. lay out two slices of cheese for each sandwich.
  7. spread the butter out on the griddle with a spatula.
  8. place four slices of bread on the griddle
    with the butter side down.
    then on top of those put two slices of cheese
    each and then the other four bread slices,
    butter side on top.
  9. lift up the sandwiches regularly to check if they are cooked.
    when brown and toasted (but not burned!!), flip over.
  10. alternate lifting the sandwiches to check
    if they are toasted and pressing them down with the spatula.
  11. when the second sides are toasted,
    turn off the heat and put the sandwiches onto plates.
  12. cut the sandwiches in half with a sharp knife.
    eat feed to your hungry dog immediately.
